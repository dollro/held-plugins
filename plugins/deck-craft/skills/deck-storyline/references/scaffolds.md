# Scaffolds by occasion

Starting patterns, not quotas. Adapt to the material; a scaffold that forces filler slides is the wrong scaffold.

## Recommendation / decision deck (consult, 8-15 slides)
1. Cover
2. Executive summary: answer, 3-4 reasons, the ask
3. Context: situation and complication in one exhibit (only if the audience needs it)
4-n. One section per supporting argument: divider with the section takeaway, 2-4 evidence slides
n+1. Options compared on the decision criteria, recommended option highlighted
n+2. Risks and how they are contained
n+3. Next steps: decisions, owners, dates
Backup: methodology, data tables, sensitivities

## Steering-committee update (consult, 6-10 slides)
1. Cover
2. Summary: status in one sentence (on track / at risk / off track and why), decisions needed today
3. Progress against plan: milestones table, deviations highlighted
4. What changed since last time (findings, scope, risks)
5-7. Deep dive on the one or two issues needing a decision
8. Decisions requested, with options and recommendation
9. Next period: actions, owners, dates

## Board update (consult or slidedoc, 8-12 slides)
1. Cover
2. Summary: what the board should know, what it must decide or note
3. KPIs vs plan and prior period (kpi_row or table), one sentence of interpretation each
4. Cash and runway (or the financial position relevant to the body)
5-7. The two or three strategic topics, each as a claim with evidence
8. Risks and mitigations
9. Resolutions requested, wording ready
Backup: full financials, pipeline, hiring

## Investment or funding decision (consult, 10-15 slides)
1. Cover
2. Recommendation and terms in one slide: invest / pass, amount, valuation, the three reasons, the key risk
3. Company in one slide: what it does, mechanism, stage
4. Why now: the change in technology, regulation or market that opens the window
5. Market: size that matters for this company, not the top-down number
6. Product and technology edge, with evidence
7. Traction and unit economics
8. Team
9. Competition: positioning against named alternatives
10. Deal economics and return path
11. Risks and what would have to be true
12. Proposed resolution and conditions

## Investor pitch, sent or presented (consult/slidedoc, 10-14 slides)
Problem → solution → why now → market → product → traction → business model → competition → team → financials → the ask and use of funds. The title of each slide states the claim; a deck sent ahead must stand without the presenter (slidedoc density).

## Report-out (consult, 5-8 slides)
1. What we set out to do and the answer
2. Results vs target
3. What worked, with evidence
4. What did not, with the cause
5. What we change next
6. Asks

## Workshop (consult, variable)
1. Objective and the output expected by the end
2. Agenda as the sequence of decisions, not topics
3. Per block: the question, the input (one exhibit), the template to fill
4. Summary of decisions and open points

## Launch, keynote, vision talk
Use deck-keynote. If the launch needs a proof section for analysts or a board, build that section with the consult rules inside the keynote deck.
