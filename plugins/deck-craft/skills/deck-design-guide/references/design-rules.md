# Default design rules

Apply when the brand guide is silent. A brand guide or master overrides any rule here. Thresholds are working limits, not targets to fill.

## Two modes, never mixed on one slide

| | consult (analyst, Tufte) | keynote (stage, Reynolds) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | prove a claim, read closely | land one idea, watched |
| Words | title ≤ 90 characters, body ≤ 5 bullets × 2 lines | ≤ 20 visible, statements 1-8 |
| Type | title 18-28 pt (master), body 12-16, labels 9-11 | statements 40-96, numbers 120-200, secondary ≥ 18 |
| Empty space | ≥ 25% | ≥ 40% |
| Density | one exhibit plus a takeaway | one element |

Pick the mode per slide and stay in it.

## Grid and alignment

1. Safe margin ≥ 0.5 in (about 5% of width) on all sides; master artwork defines its own zones.
2. Body on 2, 3, 4 or 6 columns; every element snaps to a column edge or the margin.
3. Shared edges: text blocks left-aligned on the same line; items in a row top-aligned. Off by a few pixels is the most common tell of a rushed slide.
4. Spacing on an 8 pt rhythm (0.11 in); gaps between repeated elements identical.
5. One grid for the whole deck: same title position, same body top, same source line.

## Gestalt grouping

- **Proximity**: related items close (≤ 0.2 in), unrelated far apart (≥ 0.6 in). Labels sit next to what they name.
- **Similarity**: peers share size, colour and style. Three options look like three equals.
- **Common region**: group with a shared light panel or a single hairline, not a box per item.
- **Hierarchy by position, size, then colour**: the most important element is top or left, larger, or in the accent.
- **Squint test**: blurred, the groups and the hierarchy are still visible.

## Typography

- Fonts from the master only; heading font for titles and statements, body font for everything else.
- At most 4 sizes per slide; a modular scale (ratio 1.25-1.6) across the deck.
- Line spacing 1.0-1.2 for display type, 1.1-1.4 for body.
- Line length ≤ 60-70 characters in text blocks; widen the box no further.
- Bold for emphasis; no underline; italics rarely; no ALL CAPS beyond short labels.
- Fit text by cutting words, never by shrinking below the floor (consult 12 pt, keynote 18 pt).

## Colour

- **60-30-10**: about 60% background, 30% neutrals (ink, greys), 10% brand or accent.
- **One accent per slide**, meaning "look here": the recommended option, the outlier, the quadrant you are in. If everything is accented, nothing is.
- Charts: context in greys, the point in the accent; ordered scales in shades of one hue; distinct hues only for unordered categories, at most 4.
- Same category, same colour on every slide.
- Never encode meaning by hue alone (red/green): add labels or shape.
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text (aim for 7:1 on projected slides); light grey on white is for non-essential labels only.

## Icons

- Default: no icon. Use one only when it speeds recognition of a repeated category.
- One family (line or solid, one stroke weight, one corner style), one or two sizes, monochrome in the neutral tone; the accent only on the icon that marks the important item.
- Literal metaphors (gear = operations, shield = risk); no clip art, no multicolour icons.
- If a slide has more icons than facts, it is under-substantiated.

## Imagery

- Only images the user supplied or the brand library holds; no stock photos of real people without rights.
- Full-bleed or on the master's picture placeholder; never stretched; crop, do not distort.
- Text on images only over calm areas, with sufficient contrast.

## Charts and tables

- Native, editable. No 3D, shadows, gradients, borders, background fills.
- Data labels replace axes and gridlines where they fit; value axes start at zero for bars.
- Tables: header row light panel, hairline row separators, no vertical rules, numbers right-aligned, the recommended row shaded in the accent's pale tint.
- Data-ink ≥ 80%.

## Density

- One message per slide; glance test 3-5 seconds on stage, 5-10 in a meeting.
- 3-5 visual chunks per slide (never more than 7).
- Read-ahead slidedocs: ≤ 250 words, still one point per page.

## Text hygiene

- Titles in sentence case unless the brand says otherwise.
- Numbers as digits; units consistent; thousands separator per the deck's language.
- No filler ("various", "key", "it is important to note"); no rhetorical questions on consult slides.
