# Beat roles

Each beat has one role. The role says what the beat does for the story; deck-craft and motion-craft read it to choose a slide type or a medium.

| Role | Job (one line) | Side | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `moment` | the situation everyone in the audience knows from their own week | is | – |
| `villain` | the concrete pain or waste, named and made visible | is | optional (a quote or count helps) |
| `cost` | what the villain costs, in one number | is | **required** |
| `turn` | the question that opens the better world: "What if …?" | could | – |
| `reveal` | the product named once, then silence; exactly one per narrative | could | – |
| `pillar` | one of three things the product does, parallel in form | could | – |
| `proof` | a number, result or test that makes a pillar believable | could | **required** |
| `objection` | the doubt answered before it is raised (price, privacy, fit) | could | optional |
| `versus` | old way vs new way, same unit on both sides | is → could | **required** |
| `voice` | a real, attributable person saying it in their words | could | optional (must be real) |
| `star` | the one moment they will retell: demo, before/after, startling number; exactly one | could | as the content needs |
| `outcome` | the new state of the audience's week, the promise come true | could | – |
| `close` | availability, price, the ask; exactly one, last in every cut | could | – |

Notes:
- **Required** evidence means an intake row with strength `solid` or `likely`; `missing` fails review.
- A `star` beat that shows a number needs that number's evidence like a `proof`.
- `moment` and `villain` carry the hook in no-speaker cuts: the first 2 s must make the need visible.
- Medium hints for downstream (not binding): moment / villain / outcome → footage or motion; reveal / pillar → motion graphics or a statement slide; proof / star (demo) → the real product UI, never a mockup; cost / proof numbers → one big number; voice → quote.
