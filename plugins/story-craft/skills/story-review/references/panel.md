# Expert panel

Four reviewers for a launch or keynote story. Each reads `narrative.md` and `intake.md` cold, through one lens, and gives at most three findings tied to beat IDs. Swap one seat for another story type (below).

## Seats

**Brand strategist**: does the story say one thing the brand can own?
- Could a competitor give this exact talk by swapping the name?
- Does the headline survive being the only thing someone remembers?
- Is the promise in the audience's words, not the company's?

**Narrative designer**: does the story move?
- Does tension build before the reveal, and is the reveal earned?
- Does every act alternate is and could be, or does it sag into a feature list?
- Is the STAR moment tied to the headline, and does the close land on the ask?

**Growth marketer**: will someone act afterwards?
- Is the ask one action, specific, with a place or date?
- Does proof (a voice, a number) come before the ask?
- Does each cut fit its channel: hook in 2 s for no-speaker cuts, the claim readable muted?

**Skeptical buyer**: would I believe it with my budget on the line?
- Which claim would I challenge first, and does the story answer it?
- Is any number derived, rounded up or missing its conditions?
- Which objection (price, data, switching cost, fit) is left unanswered?

## Seat swaps by story type

| Story type | Swap in | For |
|---|---|---|
| vision or strategy talk | the internal sceptic (a team lead who has seen three strategies fail) | growth marketer |
| developer or technical launch | the developer advocate (real usage, not a contrived example) | brand strategist |
| investor pitch | the investor (market, traction, why now) | growth marketer |
| recruiting or employer film | a candidate in the target role | skeptical buyer |

## Scorecard

Each seat scores 1-5 on every dimension; the table shows the average per dimension.

| Dimension | What 5 looks like | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | the one-sentence story is obvious from the statements alone | |
| Tension | the villain is felt before the product appears; the turn is a real question | |
| Proof | every claim stands on solid or honestly worded likely evidence | |
| Memorability | the headline and the STAR moment would be retold the next morning | |
| Action | the close asks for one thing the audience can do this week | |

PASS needs an average ≥ 3.5 and no dimension below 3.

## Findings format

`<seat> · <beat> · <finding> → <fix>`, e.g. `skeptical buyer · B14 · baseline is self-reported → say "pilot users estimated" or measure a baseline`.
